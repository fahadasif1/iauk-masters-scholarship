05 · Before you apply

Questions answered

If your question is not here, call us on +44 7432 289451.

Is there any cost?

No. The evening, the dinner and the draw are free, and you will not be asked to pay at any stage.

Who can enter the draw?

UK residents aged 18 or over who hold valid UK immigration permission or settled status, have an approved ticket, and check in on the evening with original photo ID.

What does the scholarship cover?

100% of the tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one Master's (postgraduate taught) course at an IAUK partner university in the UK. It does not cover living costs, accommodation, visa fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge or travel. The winner must meet the university's normal admission requirements.

When do requests close?

At 23:59 the day before each evening: Sunday 1 November for Manchester, Thursday 5 November for London and Sunday 8 November for Birmingham. A city may close earlier if all places are taken.

What identification is accepted?

An original passport or UK photo driving licence. Copies and photos on a phone are not accepted. The name must match your request.

How will I receive my ticket?

By email or SMS once your request has been reviewed. Please check your spam folder.

Can I bring someone with me?

Each ticket is for one student. Anyone else who would like to attend should submit their own request.

Terms

Scholarship draw terms

The key points: free to enter; one 100% tuition fee scholarship per event; register online by 23:59 the day before your event; attend in person with original photo ID; winner drawn at random on the night.

Read the full terms and conditions
  1. Promoter. IAUK.
  2. Free entry. The draw is free to enter. No purchase or payment is needed to enter or to win.
  3. Who can enter. UK residents aged 18 or over who hold valid UK immigration permission or settled status. Employees of IAUK, its group companies and their immediate families cannot enter.
  4. How to enter. (a) Send a seat request through this page by 23:59 (UK time) on the day before your chosen event; (b) receive an approved ticket from IAUK; (c) attend the event in person and check in with an original passport or UK photo driving licence matching the name on your request. One entry per person, for one event only.
  5. Events and closing dates. Manchester, Monday 2 November 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 1 November). London, Friday 6 November 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 5 November). Birmingham, Monday 9 November 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 8 November).
  6. Seats. Seats at each venue are limited and are given at the Promoter's discretion after reviewing each request. Not every request will receive a ticket.
  7. The prize. One prize per event (three in total): a scholarship covering 100% of the tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one postgraduate taught (Master's) course at a UK partner university of IAUK. The scholarship is valid only for one-year (full-time) Master's courses at BPP University and cannot be applied to any other university or course length.
  8. What the prize does not cover. Living costs, accommodation, visa application fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge, travel, deposits or any other cost. No cash alternative is offered. The prize cannot be transferred.
  9. Conditions of the prize. The winner must meet the university's academic and English language entry requirements, receive and accept an offer, and meet any UK immigration requirements for the course.
  10. The draw. One winner per event is drawn at random, live at the event, from guests checked in with valid ID. The winner must be present when drawn; if not, another winner is drawn.
  11. Winner announcement. The winner is announced on the night. With the winner's consent, their first name and city may be published on IAUK's website and social media.
